# Pyrhonen — Design of Rotating Electrical Machines

## Citation

Pyrhonen, J., Jokinen, T., Hrabovcova, V., "Design of Rotating Electrical Machines," Wiley, 2014.

## Why This Matters

Comprehensive textbook covering all aspects of rotating machine design including sizing, materials, windings, and thermal design.

## Key Topics Covered

- Sizing equations (D²L)
- Magnetic and electric loading
- Winding design
- Thermal design
- Material selection
- Loss modeling
