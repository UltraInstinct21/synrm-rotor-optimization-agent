# PM-Assisted SynRM (PMaSynRM)

## Operating Principle

Combines reluctance torque from flux barriers with alignment torque from embedded permanent magnets. Magnets are placed inside flux barriers.

## Status

Planned — awaiting paper ingestion for detailed content.

## Key Differences from SynRM

- Higher power factor (magnets provide some excitation)
- Higher torque density
- PM material cost and supply chain
- Demagnetization risk
